# Lucide Icons

The native VectorDrawable assets in `app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_*.xml` are
converted from Lucide 0.468.0 in the supplied design project's
`assets/lucide.js`. SVG paths and primitives are preserved; stroke width is
1.7 to match the design, with a 22 dp intrinsic size and 48 dp touch targets.

Upstream: https://lucide.dev and https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide
License source: https://unpkg.com/lucide@0.468.0/LICENSE
